About me

Curious by design

A self-starter who turned curiosity into a structured path.

I

How I got here

I have always been drawn to technology, especially how programming languages work. I began teaching myself coding and the foundations of ethical hacking through online courses that I paid for myself. As I went deeper, I realised I needed a more structured foundation, so I enrolled at Miva Open University to study Cybersecurity. There I am learning how code and network protocols fit into an enterprise-level defence strategy.

II

What sets me apart

I combine a self-starter mindset with a code-first approach to security. I want to know how things break from the inside out, so I look beyond checklists to the programming logic behind vulnerabilities. I bring the curiosity of a self-taught programmer together with the discipline of university training.
